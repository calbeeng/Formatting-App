Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Notes to Google Docs ("the app") is a personal desktop application that runs on your own computer. It has no server, no user accounts of its own, and no analytics. The developer never receives any of your data.

What the app accesses, and why

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for these permissions:

  • Google Docs: to read the Google Doc whose link you give it (its title and headings, so you can choose where to insert content) and to add your notes to that doc. The app only opens docs you point it at, and it only adds content: it never edits or deletes what's already in your doc.
  • Google Drive, files created by this app only: Google Docs can only insert a picture from a web link, so when your notes contain a diagram, the app uploads that picture to your Drive, shares it by link just long enough for Google Docs to copy it into your document, and then deletes the upload, usually within seconds. The app cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • Your email address: shown in the app so you can see which Google account is signed in.

What is stored, and where

Everything is stored only on your own computer, in the app's settings folder: your Google sign-in token (so you don't have to sign in every time), your email address, your settings, and a list of the last 10 Google Docs you used (their titles and links). The PDFs you open are read on your computer and are not uploaded anywhere, except for diagram pictures as described above.

Sharing

The app does not send your data to the developer or to any third party. Data goes only between your computer and Google, to do what you asked.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

  • Click Sign out in the app to delete the saved sign-in from your computer.
  • Revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To remove everything, delete the app and its settings folder (%APPDATA%\notes2gdoc on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/notes2gdoc on Mac).
